Set up in minutes

ZMaskFX reads the picture from OBS itself, so it never fights over your camera. It works with NVIDIA Broadcast and any other source.

Install

Run ZMaskFX and open OBS. It connects through the built-in obs-websocket.

Add the layer

ZMaskFX creates a transparent overlay on top of your camera source and keeps it aligned.

Pick a preset

Choose an effect in the OBS dock, press F9 to turn it on, and go live.

Questions

Does it work with NVIDIA Broadcast and other virtual cameras?

Yes. ZMaskFX reads the picture from the OBS source, so any source OBS can show works, including NVIDIA Broadcast.

What do I need?

Windows 10/11 and OBS Studio 28 or newer with obs-websocket enabled (included in OBS). A regular gaming PC is plenty.

Can I use my licence on two PCs?

One licence works on one PC at a time. Use "Deactivate on this PC" in the app to move it to another computer whenever you want.

Is the WoW addon safe?

It is free, open source and only displays information about your own spells. It does not automate anything or read other players. ZMaskFX is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

What does Early Access mean?

ZMaskFX is already working and in use, and we keep adding effects, games and fixes. Early Access buyers get the lowest price and every update. If something does not work on your setup, write to us and we will fix it or refund you.
