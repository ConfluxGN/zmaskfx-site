How it works

Set up in minutes

ZMaskFX reads the picture from OBS itself, so it never fights over your camera. It works with NVIDIA Broadcast and any other source.

  1. 1

    Install and open OBS

    Install ZMaskFX and open OBS. It connects on its own.

  2. 2

    Add the layer

    Add the "ZMaskFX Overlay" source to your scenes; ZMaskFX keeps it aligned with your camera.

  3. 3

    Pick a preset

    Choose an effect in the ZMaskFX panel inside OBS, press F9 to turn it on, and go live.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work with NVIDIA Broadcast and other virtual cameras?

Yes. ZMaskFX reads the picture from the OBS source, so any source OBS can show works, including NVIDIA Broadcast.

What do I need?

Windows 10/11 and OBS Studio 28 or newer with obs-websocket enabled (included in OBS). A regular gaming PC is plenty.

Can I use my license on two PCs?

One license works on one PC at a time. To switch, just activate your key on the new PC: the license moves there by itself and the old PC goes back to Free. Reinstalling on the same PC never counts.

Is the WoW addon safe?

It is free, open source and only displays information about your own spells. It does not automate anything or read other players. ZMaskFX is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Yes. Windows shows that notice for programs from small independent studios that are not downloaded by millions yet: it means “unknown publisher”, not that a threat was found. Click “More info” and then “Run anyway”. The installer does not ask for administrator rights and only installs into its own folder; uninstalling removes it completely.

How can I check what ZMaskFX sends?

You do not have to take our word for it. With ZMaskFX running, open Windows Resource Monitor (Network tab) or any firewall: you will only see our licence and effect pack server (Cloudflare) and updates (GitHub). Your video never leaves your PC. If your antivirus has doubts, you can also scan the installer on VirusTotal. Details in the privacy policy.
